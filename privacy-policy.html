PRIVACY POLICY

Allen Psychiatry

Effective Date: July 24, 2026  |  Last Updated: July 24, 2026

1. Overview

Allen Psychiatry ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website located at https://allenpsychiatry.net (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information on the Site.

We do not collect, store, process, sell, or share any Personal Information from visitors to this Site. This includes, without limitation: names, email addresses, physical addresses, phone numbers, payment information, account credentials, device identifiers, IP addresses, geolocation data, cookies, browsing history, or any other data that identifies or could reasonably be linked to you.

The Site does not use cookies, web beacons, pixel tags, session replay tools, analytics software, advertising trackers, or any similar technology. The Site does not include embedded third-party content (such as social media widgets, maps, fonts, or video players) that would transmit visitor data to a third party.

2. No Tracking, Monitoring, or Interception of Communications

In connection with the California Invasion of Privacy Act (California Penal Code §§ 630-638), we confirm that the Site does not:

  • Record, intercept, or eavesdrop on any communication between you and the Site or any third party;
  • Use session replay, keystroke logging, mouse-tracking, or chatbot technology that captures visitor interactions;
  • Deploy tracking pixels, software development kits (SDKs), or similar tools that share visitor data with third parties; or
  • Permit any third party to monitor, record, or intercept communications occurring on or through the Site.

If this changes in the future — for example, if we add live chat, analytics, or advertising technology — we will update this Policy and implement appropriate consent mechanisms (such as a consent banner or in-product disclosure) before any such technology becomes active.

3. Information You Provide Directly

The Site does not contain forms, account registration, newsletter sign-ups, comment sections, or other mechanisms for submitting information to us. If you contact us outside the Site (for example, by sending an email to an address we publish), any information you choose to share in that communication is governed by the policies, if any, of the email or communication service you use — not by this Policy — and we will use it only to respond to you.

4. Server Logs and Hosting

Our hosting provider may automatically and temporarily record limited technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and access times) in standard server logs for security and operational purposes. These logs are not used to identify individual visitors, are not combined with other information, and are not shared with third parties except as required by law.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the Site does not collect any information from any visitor, it does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 or any other age, and no additional measures under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) are applicable.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Because we do not collect Personal Information, there is no Personal Information for us to access, delete, correct, or sell.

7. Changes to This Policy

If our data practices change — including the addition of any analytics, cookies, tracking, or communication-monitoring technology referenced in Section 2 — we will revise this Policy, update the "Last Updated" date above, and, where required by law, provide additional notice or obtain consent before the change takes effect.

SMS Privacy Policy

📱 SMS Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2025

Information We Collect

When you provide your phone number for SMS communications with Allen Psychiatry, we collect:

  • Your phone number
  • Message delivery status and timestamps
  • Message content (appointment reminders, confirmations, practice updates)
  • Your responses to our messages
How We Use Your Information

We use your phone number and SMS data exclusively to:

  • Send appointment reminders and confirmations
  • Provide important practice updates and notifications
  • Send treatment-related communications when necessary
  • Improve our communication services
Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your phone number or SMS data to third parties. We may share information only:

  • With your explicit written consent
  • As required by law or legal process
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our patients
Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your SMS data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Secure storage protocols
  • Limited access to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security assessments and updates
Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying "STOP" to any message
  • Request access to your SMS data
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal and medical record retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent at any time
Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. You may receive:

  • Appointment reminders (typically 24-48 hours before your appointment)
  • Appointment confirmations
  • Practice updates (infrequent)
  • Treatment-related communications (as needed)
Opt-Out Instructions

To stop receiving SMS messages:

  • Reply "STOP" to any message
  • Call us at (801) 555-0123
  • Email us at info@allenpsychiatry.net
