## 🎯 **SEO BEST PRACTICES FOR ALT TEXT**

### **Do:**
- ✅ Describe what the image shows AND its context
- ✅ Include relevant location keywords (Provo, Utah, Utah County, etc.)
- ✅ Include service keywords (mental health, psychiatric, therapy, etc.)
- ✅ Keep it concise (125 characters or less)
- ✅ Write for humans first, SEO second
- ✅ Be specific and descriptive

### **Don't:**
- ❌ Use generic descriptions ("image", "photo", "picture")
- ❌ Keyword stuff unnaturally
- ❌ Leave alt text empty (unless decorative)
- ❌ Use file names as alt text
- ❌ Make it too long (over 125 characters)
