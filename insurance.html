Insurance Accepted

We accept most major insurance plans. Verify your coverage and learn about payment options.

Accepted Insurance Plans

Allen Psychiatry is committed to making mental health care accessible. We accept the following insurance plans:

  • Select Health
  • EMI
  • DMBA
  • PEHP
  • Blue Cross/Blue Shield

Verify Your Coverage

Coverage varies by plan and individual policy. We recommend:

  • Contact your insurance provider to verify that Allen Psychiatry is in-network for your specific plan
  • Check your benefits to understand your copay, deductible, and coverage limits
  • Call our office at (385) 504-1334 to discuss payment options and insurance questions

Payment Options

In addition to insurance, we also accept:

  • Cash payments
  • Credit card payments
  • Debit card payments

If you have questions about payment options or need to discuss a payment plan, please contact our office. We're here to help make mental health care accessible.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept my insurance plan?

We accept Select Health, EMI, DMBA, PEHP, and Blue Cross/Blue Shield. However, coverage can vary based on your specific plan. We recommend contacting your insurance provider directly to verify that Allen Psychiatry is in-network for your plan.

What if my insurance isn't listed?

If your insurance plan isn't listed, please call our office at (385) 504-1334. We may still be able to work with your insurance provider, or we can discuss other payment options.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring your insurance card, a photo ID, a list of current medications, and any pre-visit paperwork that was sent to you.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we understand that mental health care is important and want to make it accessible. Please contact our office to discuss payment plan options that work for you.

Ready to Schedule an Appointment?

If you have questions about insurance coverage or payment options, we're here to help. Contact us today to verify your coverage and schedule your appointment.

SMS Privacy Policy

📱 SMS Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2025

Information We Collect

When you provide your phone number for SMS communications with Allen Psychiatry, we collect:

  • Your phone number
  • Message delivery status and timestamps
  • Message content (appointment reminders, confirmations, practice updates)
  • Your responses to our messages
How We Use Your Information

We use your phone number and SMS data exclusively to:

  • Send appointment reminders and confirmations
  • Provide important practice updates and notifications
  • Send treatment-related communications when necessary
  • Improve our communication services
Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your phone number or SMS data to third parties. We may share information only:

  • With your explicit written consent
  • As required by law or legal process
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our patients
Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your SMS data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Secure storage protocols
  • Limited access to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security assessments and updates
Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying "STOP" to any message
  • Request access to your SMS data
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal and medical record retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent at any time
Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. You may receive:

  • Appointment reminders (typically 24-48 hours before your appointment)
  • Appointment confirmations
  • Practice updates (infrequent)
  • Treatment-related communications (as needed)
Opt-Out Instructions

To stop receiving SMS messages:

  • Reply "STOP" to any message
  • Call us at (801) 555-0123
  • Email us at info@allenpsychiatry.net
